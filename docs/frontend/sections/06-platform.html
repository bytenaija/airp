Memories

Institutional knowledge AIRP built while investigating issues and analyzing infrastructure.

Checkout latency spikes follow deploys of checkout-service

Three of the last four p99 latency incidents started within 20 minutes of a checkout-service deploy. The canary stage now watches p99 for 15 minutes before promoting.

Source: investigation INC-2418 · Created Sep 30, 2026 · Used 6 times

Cloudflare cache purge fixes stale pricing pages

When the pricing page serves content older than the last CMS publish, purging the Cloudflare cache for /pricing/* resolves it without a redeploy.

Source: investigation INC-2391 · Created Sep 26, 2026 · Used 3 times

Postgres connection pool saturates before CPU does

On db-primary, pool wait time is the leading indicator of overload. Scale the pool before adding CPU; the runbook RB-14 encodes this.

Source: human note by E. Olumese · Created Sep 21, 2026 · Used 11 times

Fly.io regions fra and ams share a noisy neighbor pattern

Disk I/O contention in fra/ams correlates with elevated error rates on edge-gateway. Prefer iad or lhr for latency-sensitive workloads.

Source: investigation INC-2355 · Created Sep 17, 2026 · Used 2 times
